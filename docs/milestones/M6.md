# M6: free-form designer

Goal: the editor grows from "auto layout with tweaks" into a designer where any page (and the
cover) can be laid out by hand on the same page model: move and resize photo slots, add text
boxes, snap to guides, undo/redo, keyboard control. Templates become starting points, not
constraints. Nothing about rendering or the viewer changes because both draw from the same
`Page`/`SlotContent` model.

Read `CLAUDE.md` first. Everything under "Existing seams" already works; build on it.

## Existing seams

- `Page`, `SlotContent`, `Crop`, `BookCover` in `packages/shared/src/book.ts`; templates and
  `SlotSpec` (`x, y, w, h` in trim-width/height fractions, `role`, `aspect`, `bleed`) in
  `packages/layout/src/templates.ts` and `packages/shared/src/template.ts`. `slotToPx` in
  `packages/layout/src/index.ts` is the one place that turns template fractions into page pixels
  (origin = top-left of the bleed box).
- `PageView` (`packages/pages/src/PageView.tsx`) positions slots from the template geometry; the
  renderer (`print.tsx`), the preview PNGs and the editor reuse it. `CoverView` does the same for
  the cover sheet through `coverSlotPx` (back cover x in [-1, 0], front in [0, 1], spine inserted at 0).
- Pure editor operations with tests: `apps/web/src/lib/editor.ts` (`swapSlots`, `removePhoto`,
  `addPhoto`, `movePage`, `setTemplate`, `setCrop`, `setSlotText`, ...). Undo in `Editor.tsx` is a
  `History` of `Page[]` snapshots (`historyPush/Undo/Redo`, limit 100) with no coalescing; the cover
  is edited separately on the book page (`components/CoverCard.tsx`) with its own draft state.
- Openers are fixed pages (`isFlexiblePage`); `layoutBook` (`apps/server/src/books/layout.ts`)
  replaces every page on re-layout and keeps `book.cover`.
- `preflightBook` (`packages/layout/src/preflight.ts`) computes resolution and caption safety from
  template geometry.

## Deliverables

### 1. Geometry overrides and ad-hoc slots (shared + layout)

- `SlotFrame` in `packages/shared/src/book.ts`: `{ x, y, w, h, rotation?, z? }` in template units
  (fractions of the trim width for `x`/`w`, trim height for `y`/`h`; the cover keeps its
  back/front convention). `SlotContent.frame?: SlotFrame` overrides the template slot's box.
- Ad-hoc slots: `SlotContent.role?: 'photo' | 'text'` marks a slot the template does not know
  (ids `photo-<uuid>` / `text-<uuid>`); such slots must carry a `frame`. `SlotContent.style?:
  TextStyle` (`{ font: 'display' | 'body', sizePt, align, italic }`) styles text boxes from the theme.
- `Page.custom?: true` on any page with a frame or ad-hoc slot. `pageSlots(page)` in
  `packages/layout/src/design.ts` returns the effective `SlotSpec[]` (template slots with frames
  applied, then ad-hoc slots) and is what `PageView`, `preflightBook` and the editor iterate.
- `layoutBook` keeps custom pages: their photos are left out of the paginate input and the pages are
  merged back at their previous index (`mergeCustomPages` in `packages/layout/src/paginate.ts`,
  openers re-aligned to a verso, page count re-normalised, chapter `startsAtPage` recomputed).
  "Reset page to template" (`resetPage`) clears frames, ad-hoc slots and the flag.

### 2. Snapping and constraints (`packages/layout/src/snap.ts`, pure)

- `pageSnapLines(format, side)` -> trim and bleed edges, safety band (gutter safety on the spine
  side), page centre; `coverSnapLines(format, geometry)` -> the same per cover panel plus the sheet
  and spine edges; `slotSnapLines(rects)` -> other slots' edges and centres (the editor adds them).
  `snapMove(rect, lines, threshold)` moves a rect onto the nearest line per axis (edges and centre)
  and returns which lines hit; `snapResize` snaps only the dragged edges; `resizeBy` applies a drag
  with optional aspect lock and a minimum size.
- `clampToBounds` keeps at least `MIN_INSIDE_IN` (0.25 in) of a box inside the bleed box, so nothing
  is placed fully off the page. Print safety stays advisory (ppi badges, preflight).
- `snapAngle` snaps rotation to multiples of 15 degrees within 3 degrees.

### 3. Rendering (`packages/pages`)

- `PageView` and `CoverView` draw every slot from `pageSlots`/`coverSlots`, apply `rotation` with a
  CSS transform around the box centre and `z` as `z-index`, and render ad-hoc text boxes with
  `textBoxStyle(theme, style)`. Nothing else changes; the PDF and the preview PNGs follow.
- Editor hooks: `onSlotPointerDown(slot, content, event)` on every slot (text slots included),
  keyboard activation (Enter/Space) on focused slots, `selectedSlotId` works for text slots.

### 4. Editor operations (`apps/web/src/lib/designer.ts`, pure, tested)

- `setFrame`, `nudge` (1/10 in, shift 1/2 in), `setZ` (front/back), `addTextBox`, `addPhotoBox`,
  `setTextStyle`, `duplicateSlot` (text boxes only: a photo may sit in one slot), `deleteSlot`
  (ad-hoc slots vanish; a template photo slot empties), `resetPage`. Each marks the page custom.
- `addPhoto` on a custom page fills an empty slot or adds an ad-hoc photo box instead of refitting;
  `removePhoto` on a custom page empties the slot instead of refitting.
- The same operations run on the cover through `withCoverSlots`, so `Editor.tsx` edits
  `{ pages, cover }` as one document.
- History becomes a stack over `{ pages, cover }` with `historyPush(h, next, coalesceKey?)`:
  consecutive pushes with the same key within 800 ms replace the present entry (nudges, typing).
  Drags commit once on pointer-up.

### 5. Editor UI (`apps/web/src/components/Designer.tsx` + `Editor.tsx`)

- `DesignOverlay` draws the selection box, eight resize handles, a rotation handle and the active
  snap lines over the page/cover at the canvas scale, and runs the pointer interactions (pointer
  capture; aspect locked for photos unless Alt is held; Shift disables snapping).
- Right panel: the selection section gains position and size in inches, rotation, z-order,
  duplicate, delete, and text content and style for text slots; "Text box" adds one to the focused
  page (a tray click adds a photo box on a custom page); "Reset to template" on custom pages; a
  "Custom" chip on the page header, a badge on the page and a mark in the filmstrip.
- Cover view: the filmstrip gains a "Cover" entry (`?view=cover`) that shows `CoverView` with the
  spine drawn as a guide and the same tools; the book page's cover card links here.
- Keyboard: Tab through slots, Enter selects, arrows nudge a selected slot (else page through
  spreads), `[`/`]` z-order, Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete, T adds a text box, Ctrl+Z/Y, `?` opens the
  shortcut overlay. Visible focus rings on slots, filmstrip and tray.

### 6. Docs

- `docs/design.md`: designer conventions (units, custom pages, snapping, constraints, shortcuts).
- `docs/print-specs.md`: overrides are part of the page model; preflight reads effective geometry.
- README status paragraph and roadmap row; this file's "Delivered" section.

## Judgement calls already made

- No new dependency for drag/drop: pointer events on the existing absolutely positioned page.
  Snapping math lives in `packages/layout/src/snap.ts` (pure, unit-tested).
- Custom pages survive "Re-layout" untouched; "Reset page to template" clears overrides.
- Print safety is advisory (badges), never a hard block, except that nothing may be placed
  fully outside the bleed box.
- Frames stay in template units rather than inches so a custom page keeps its proportions across
  formats and the cover keeps its back/front convention across page counts (spine width).
- Ad-hoc photo boxes exist (not only text boxes): on a custom page "add photo" must not refit the
  page to a template, so it needs somewhere to put the photo.
- Text boxes get four style knobs (font face from the theme, size in points, alignment, italic);
  colour follows the theme ink (paper on cover photos).

## Acceptance criteria

- Overrides round-trip through the API and render identically in the editor, PNG preview and PDF:
  a Chromium test moves one photo into a known quadrant and checks the preview PNG pixels there
  and elsewhere; the PDF of the same page renders without warnings.
- All editor operations have pure-function tests; undo/redo restores exact state, coalesced
  nudges undo as one step.
- Re-layout keeps a custom page and does not place its photos twice.
- Preflight reports resolution from the frame, not the template slot.
- The fake-Immich manual flow demonstrates move, resize, text box, snap and keyboard-only editing.

## Delivered

Landed 2026-09-14 as specified above, with these deviations and details:

- `TextStyle` is `{ font, sizePt, align, italic }` with zod defaults, so a stored style is always
  complete; a template text slot with a `style` switches to `textBoxStyle` entirely (a "Use the
  template's own style" link removes it). Colour is not a knob: ink from the theme, paper on a cover photo.
- Template text slots are never deleted: Delete stores an empty override (`text: ''`) which hides
  them; on the cover an empty text field removes the override so the fallback (book title, dates)
  returns. Template photo slots are emptied, keeping their frame.
- `addPhoto` gained an optional `format` argument (default: the 8.5 in square) so the tray can drop
  into a custom page without a second call site; `removePhoto` on a custom page empties the slot.
- Re-layout warns `N hand-designed page(s) kept as is/they are` and passes `targetPages - custom`
  to `paginate`; a custom title page suppresses the automatic one. Chapter `startsAtPage` is
  recomputed after the merge.
- The Chromium acceptance test (`apps/server/src/books/designer.test.ts`) samples preview PNG
  pixels inside and outside the frame and checks that the text box inks its area; the print PDF of
  the same book renders with no warnings. A pixel-for-pixel PDF-vs-PNG compare was not added:
  both come from one `PageView` markup, which `packages/pages` asserts byte-identical between
  `renderPrintDocument` and the editor render.
- Keyboard: `t` (no modifier) adds a text box, `?` toggles the help, arrows nudge only with a
  selection so paging still works; the Browser pane could not deliver real key events, so the
  keyboard flow was verified with dispatched `KeyboardEvent`s plus the unit tests.
- Not done: dragging tray photos onto the page (click still places them), multi-selection,
  rotation snapping to other boxes, and a cover-specific safety check in preflight. All four landed
  in M7 (see M7.md, "Deferred items").

## Review fixes (2026-09-15)

- A slot sent behind everything stays above the paper in print (stacking context on the page).
- Hand-placed cover frames draw linearly (`coverFrameIn`), so the editor's inverse round-trips and a
  box no longer grows by a spine width per drag; `CoverCard` keeps the hero's frame when the photo changes.
- A hand-designed chapter opener keeps its chapter open on re-layout instead of a second spread.
- Nudges and typed positions obey the off-page clamp; aspect-locked resizes snap; a drag commits
  once (no state-updater side effects); `PageView`/`CoverView` are memoised.
